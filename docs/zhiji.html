Name · Slogan · Origin

名称、题语与出处 / Name, Slogan, and Classical Origin

中英文并列呈现，突出“知几驾驶”与 AMC-Drive 的内在对应关系。

中文

名称：知几驾驶 · AMC-Drive

“知几”取意《周易》“知几其神乎，几者动之微”。这里的“几”，不是宏大的终局，而是变化将成未成之际的细微征兆。对于自动驾驶而言，不同轨迹并不必然意味着不同未来；真正重要的是，能否辨识那些几何上接近、但在交互后果上已开始分化的关键差异。

English

Name: Zhiji Driving · AMC-Drive

The term Zhiji is inspired by the line from the Book of Changes: “To perceive the subtle incipient sign is close to the divine.” In this page, the subtle sign means the tiny divergence that may later produce a decisively different consequence. Different trajectories do not necessarily imply different futures; what matters is whether the method can detect the key divergence hidden beneath geometric similarity.

中文

题语：辨其同，存其异；不求多，而求当。

此句取意于《论语》“和而不同”与《中庸》“执其两端，用其中于民”。在 AMC-Drive 中，这并非装饰性的文学点缀，而是方法思想的直接表达：面对固定预算的多模态输出，不应将所有几何差异都等同为有价值的 future，也不应因局部相似而粗暴合并那些后果不同的候选。

English

Slogan: Distinguish what is effectively the same, preserve what is truly different; seek not more, but what is appropriate.

The slogan echoes the spirit of the Analects and the Doctrine of the Mean. Under a fixed multimodal budget, AMC-Drive should neither mistake every geometric variation for a meaningful future, nor merge together candidates whose downstream consequences remain importantly different.

辨其同识别 consequence-equivalent proposals，而不是把每一个坐标扰动都当作独立未来。
存其异保留 interaction timing、collision margin、progress 等后果层面真正不同的候选。
不求多不盲目追求更大的候选数量，而是尊重固定预算中的结构组织问题。
而求当追求与场景复杂度相称、对决策真正有用的有效多模态。
Method Overview

方法主线 / Method Mainline

从“生成更多轨迹”转向“更好地使用固定预算的候选容量”。

中文

AMC-Drive 关注的不是名义上有多少条轨迹，而是在固定的候选预算中，到底有多少条 future 在后果层面是有效区分的。方法将每一条候选轨迹视为一条待验证的 action，然后借助冻结的动作条件预测表征观察其 future consequences，通过候选之间的 consequence affinity 估计冗余关系，再用辅助校准目标重组织 proposal set，使固定预算更集中地承载真正不同的决策机会。

English

AMC-Drive asks not how many trajectories are produced, but how many of them correspond to genuinely distinct future consequences under a fixed proposal budget. Each candidate trajectory is treated as an action to be verified. A frozen action-conditioned predictive representation inspects its future consequences, pairwise consequence affinity reveals redundancy structure, and an auxiliary calibration objective reorganizes the proposal set so that the same budget can better represent decision-relevant opportunities.

Philosophical Reflection

哲学思考 / Philosophical Reflection

让中文命名、项目题语与算法结构形成一一对应。

知几 / Perceiving the Subtle

方法关注“几何上看似相近、后果上却开始分化”的微妙未来差异；这正是“知几”在现代算法中的技术对应。

辨其同 / Distinguishing Sameness

许多候选只是名义上的多模态，却在后果层面近乎可互换。识别这种同，是减少无效冗余的前提。

存其异 / Preserving Difference

安全相关、决策相关的 future 不应因局部几何接近而被合并。存其异，是对真正 planning choice 的保留。

不求多，而求当 / Not More, but Appropriate

多模态的价值不在表面数量，而在与场景复杂度相匹配、与下游决策真正相关的有效容量。

中文

因此，“知几驾驶”并不是一个独立于技术之外的文学包装，而是对 AMC-Drive 方法论的另一种凝练表述：在固定预算的多模态规划中，我们追求的不是表面繁复，而是对关键未来差异的准确感知、合理组织与审慎保留。

English

For this reason, Zhiji Driving is not merely a literary wrapper outside the method. It is a compact restatement of AMC-Drive itself: under a fixed multimodal planning budget, the goal is not superficial abundance, but precise perception, principled organization, and careful preservation of decision-relevant future differences.
